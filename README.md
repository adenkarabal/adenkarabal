I work on patterns and the relations between them, causal structures and links.

The work is a way of reading the world through recurring causal processes rather than through topics. Two parts of it are public.

**[causal-mechanism-registry](https://github.com/adenkarabal/causal-mechanism-registry)** — thirty-two bounded causal mechanisms for historical crisis research. Elicited from a model's reading of hundreds of cases, filtered by recurrence across them, gated, frozen and versioned. [Why this method](https://github.com/adenkarabal/causal-mechanism-registry/blob/main/WHY.md) states the problem it answers: how a person puts a question whose answer they cannot describe in advance, without their own knowledge becoming the limit of the answer.

**[adenkarabal.com](https://adenkarabal.com)** — where the dated readings are published. The registry is open under CC BY 4.0; the readings are not.
